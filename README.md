# Bike-Sales-Dashboard-Excel-
An Excel report that analyzes bike sales under various criteria such as commute distance, age group and gender. This helps identify strong customer bases and areas for improvement.

## Tasks Performed

1) Compared income groups by gender by contrasting those who bought bikes versus those who didn't
 People who bought bikes tended to have more income than those who didn’t regardless of gender

2) Compared customers who bought (or didn’t buy) bikes based on their average commute distance
People whose average commute distance was less than 5 miles were more likely to buy bikes than those for whom it was more than 5 miles

3) Compared customers who bought (or didn’t buy) bikes based on their age group
The number of bike purchases made by middle aged customers (aged between 30 to 60 years) was far more than the other age groups combined
Middle aged customers also were more likely to buy bikes than not, unlike youth and senior customers

4) Added slicers to the visualisation tab to help filter customers by marital status, education and region
